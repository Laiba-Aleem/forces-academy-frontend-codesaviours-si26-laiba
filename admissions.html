Eligibility

Who Can Apply

Pakistan Military Army Long Course Preparation
1.5–2 Months FSc / FA / ICS
  • Age: 17 to 22 years
  • FSc minimum 50% marks
  • Physically fit and medically sound
  • Must be eligible for initial test call
Armed Forces Nursing Services (AFNS)
2–4.5 Months FSc Pre-Medical Female
  • Gender: Female only
  • Age: 17 to 24 years
  • FSc Pre-Medical minimum 60% marks
  • Unmarried at time of application
Lady Cadet Course (LCC) Preparation
1–1.5 Months Female Graduates
  • Gender: Female only
  • Age: 21 to 26 years
  • Bachelor's degree any discipline
  • Unmarried Pakistani national
PAF and Pak Navy Initial Test Prep
1.5 Months FSc Students
  • Age: 16 to 22 years
  • FSc Pre-Engineering or Pre-Medical
  • Minimum 60% marks
  • Good eyesight preferred
ISSB Obstacle & GTO Course Preparation
5 Days–2 Weeks ISSB Shortlisted
  • Must have ISSB call letter
  • Any educational background
  • No age restriction
  • Physically fit for outdoor tasks
Medical Cadet Course Preparation
2–3 Months FSc Pre-Medical
  • Age: 17 to 22 years
  • FSc Pre-Medical minimum 60% marks
  • Pakistani national
  • Physically and medically fit

Checklist

Required Documents

Primary Identification
  • Candidate's CNIC / B-Form 1 copy
  • Guardian's CNIC 1 copy
  • Passport-sized photographs 2–4
Academic Certificates
  • Matric / O-Level result card Original + 1 copy
  • Intermediate (FSc/FA/ICS) result card
  • Graduation degree / transcript If applicable
Additional Requirements
  • ISSB Call Letter / Rejection Letter
  • Shuhada Certificate / Kinship proof If applicable
All documents must be submitted at the time of registration. Incomplete applications will not be processed.
