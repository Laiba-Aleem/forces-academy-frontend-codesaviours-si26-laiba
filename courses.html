PMA Long Course Preparation

Prepare for Pakistan Army regular commission with complete test and ISSB training.

Armed Forces Nursing Services (AFNS)

Preparation for Army Nursing entry tests, interviews, and ISSB.

  • Duration: 2-4.5 Months
  • For: FSc Pre-Medical Female Students

Lady Cadet Course (LCC)

Comprehensive Preparation for direct commission as a Lady Cadet through SSRC with our expert and specialized ISSB training.

  • For: Female Graduates
