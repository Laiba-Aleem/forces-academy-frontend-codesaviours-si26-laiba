WHO WE ARE

A Legacy of Leadership & Discipline

Forces Academy Faisalabad is a premier institute dedicated to preparing aspiring students for careers in Pakistan's Armed Forces.

Students develop confidence, communication, decision-making, and leadership abilities through structured training, personality development and expert mentorship, preparing them for every stage of military selection process.

Our History

Built on Discipline Since 2018

Founded in 2018 by former ISSB selectors and retired armed forces officers, Forces Academy Faisalabad began with a simple mission: to provide professional military preparation under the guidance of experienced mentors.

With 500+ commissioned officers and a strong record of successful candidates, Forces Academy continues to inspire and prepare the next generation of future leaders.

Our Mission

To nurture disciplined, confident, and capable young leaders by combining strong academics with character building, physical fitness, and moral values.

Our Vision

To be recognized as the leading academy in the region for producing well-rounded students who serve their nation with honor and integrity.
